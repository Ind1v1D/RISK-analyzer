INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Northwind
Studios LLC ("Client") and Jordan Reyes ("Contractor").

1. SERVICES. Contractor will design and develop the Client's marketing website,
including up to eight pages, source files, and basic search-engine
optimization setup, in accordance with the schedule agreed by the parties.

2. TERM. This Agreement begins on the effective date and continues until the
services are completed. Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for
any reason or no reason, upon written notice. Contractor must provide at least
thirty (30) days' written notice before ceasing any work in progress.

3. COMPENSATION. Client will pay Contractor a total fee of $6,000, payable
within ninety (90) days following Client's acceptance of the final
deliverable. Acceptance shall be determined by Client in Client's sole and
absolute discretion. Time is of the essence with respect to Contractor's
delivery deadlines.

4. CANCELLATION. In the event this Agreement is terminated for any reason
whatsoever, Contractor hereby waives any and all fees for work performed, work
in progress, or cancellation, and Client's sole liability shall be limited to
amounts already paid prior to termination.

5. REVISIONS. Contractor will make all revisions requested by Client, without
limitation as to number, scope, or hours expended, at no additional cost.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. All work product, including without limitation any
pre-existing tools, code libraries, design systems, templates, and know-how
used or created by Contractor in connection with the services, shall be the
sole and exclusive property of Client. Contractor hereby assigns all right,
title, and interest therein worldwide in perpetuity.

7. INDEMNIFICATION. Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless
Client and its officers, directors, and employees from and against any and all
claims, damages, losses, and expenses arising out of or related to the
services, and Contractor's liability under this paragraph shall be unlimited.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY. Contractor shall keep secret and confidential all
information of Client, including without limitation information that is
publicly available or generally known in the industry.

9. NON-COMPETE. For two (2) years following completion of the services,
Contractor shall not perform website design or development services for any
business or individual located anywhere in the United States.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State
of Delaware, and Contractor consents to the exclusive jurisdiction of its
courts for any dispute arising under this Agreement.

11. EXPENSES. Contractor shall bear all costs of performing the services,
including equipment, software, and travel.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between
the parties and supersedes all prior understandings. This Agreement may be
amended only in a writing signed by both parties.
